Music recommendations by text

This educational project uses an AI model to interpret a mood or activity sent by SMS and responds with matching tracks and Spotify links. It is designed for a small, invited testing group, not public marketing.

Setup status: SMS enrollment and reply delivery are not yet fully operational. Do not publish this page as evidence of a working consent process until the START/STOP/HELP behavior described below has been implemented and verified.

How to opt in (planned process)

  1. Read this page and the linked Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
  2. If invited to test, text START to (582) 233-3339 to request enrollment.
  3. After receiving a confirmation, text your mood (for example, “I want upbeat surfing music”) to request Spotify song recommendations.
  4. Reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

Consent disclosure: By texting START, you request automated non-marketing replies from EGR404 Mood Playlist Bot. Message frequency varies based on your requests. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is voluntary and not a condition of purchase.

No recurring promotional messages are planned.

Example message exchange (illustration only)

Participant → START

Bot → EGR404 Mood Playlist Bot: You are subscribed for on-demand Spotify suggestions. Msg frequency varies; msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
