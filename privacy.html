Purpose and scope

lab5 is a limited-access educational SMS project. Invited participants text descriptions of moods or activities and receive automatically generated music suggestions that link to Spotify.

Information processed

When the service is used, information may include your phone number, the SMS text you send, message times and delivery status, any opt-in or opt-out preference, AI-generated music-search phrases, and Spotify track results. Service providers may also generate operational and diagnostic records.

How information is used

Information is used to receive requests, identify permitted participants, generate music-search queries, retrieve Spotify search results, send requested replies, honor opt-out requests, investigate errors, and document course testing. The service is not intended to send advertisements.

Service providers

Twilio transports messages and may retain messaging records. OpenAI processes submitted message content to produce a music-search query. Spotify receives music search queries and supplies track metadata and links. These providers handle information under their own applicable terms and policies.

Retention and access

The lab5 operator uses a local SQLite database to record whether a testing number has opted in, if the optional sample server provided with this website is deployed. Twilio maintains its own messaging and delivery records under its policies. The operator may inspect diagnostic console output during development. Before publication, confirm how long any local and provider records are retained and update this statement to match the deployed implementation.

Sharing and marketing

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or consent with third parties for marketing purposes. Information needed to operate the requested service is processed by the providers described above. We do not use this service to send unsolicited marketing messages.

Your choices

Once keyword handling is configured, reply STOP to opt out and HELP for assistance. To request access to or deletion of information controlled by the project operator, contact ibrahim.tahoun@uri.edu. Some platform records may be subject to provider retention or legal requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the project's operation changes. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
